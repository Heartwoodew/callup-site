Mission · FAQ

Your questions, answered.

How does the free trial work?

You get 14 days of CallUp free, with every feature. Open app.callupapp.com.au and tap Create a company. No card needed to start.

How much does it cost?

$50/month + GST, which includes 1 person. Each extra staff member is $5/month + GST. The pricing calculator shows your total.

Can I cancel?

Yes, anytime. There's no lock-in contract.

Can I export my records?

Download your invoices as CSV files to upload into any bookkeeping software.

Does it track my crew's location?

Yes. CallUp is GPS-enabled. While someone is clocked on with CallUp open, their phone shares its GPS position about once a minute, or sooner if they move more than 50 metres. Managers and admins see a live crew map on the home screen showing who's clocked on, when each phone last updated, and today's job. Supervisors and crew don't see it. Only the latest position is kept, and clocking off removes it. One location is also saved on the timesheet at clock on, clock off and breaks. Crew can say no to location and still clock on, and sharing stops when the phone is locked or CallUp isn't on screen. See Live crew locations.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. CallUp is built phone-first and runs in your phone's web browser.

Who sees what?

Admins set staff permissions. Crew see their own jobs, and rates and quotes stay with authorised roles.

Who is CallUp for?

Small trade and earthworks crews in Brisbane and SEQ.

What trades does it suit?

Earthworks, landscaping, concreting, fencing, carpentry, tiling, plumbing, electrical and concrete cutting. Pick one or several when you set up your company and each gets its own job types, job titles and quote pack. You can add or remove trades later. See Trades.

Can the crew see quotes and prices?

No. Rates and quotes stay with supervisors, managers and admins, and job documents such as quotes and invoices can be kept Office only.

Who's behind CallUp?

Heartwood Earthworx Pty Ltd, a Brisbane earthworks business. See About.

How do I get in touch?
