Features

Everything the crew needs, on the phone.

Every feature is included in the one plan.

Invoices, timesheets, reimbursements and jobs export as CSV files that can be imported into your accounting software.

Weekly planner on desktop

Set up the crew's whole week from the office computer.

  • Plan the week's jobs on a big screen
  • Assign crew and machines to each day
  • Changes show up on everyone's phone

Jobs & run sheets

Plan the work and put the day's run sheet on every phone.

  • Job details, address and scope in one place
  • Daily run sheet so the crew know where they're going
  • Staff see the jobs they're on

Job photos

Site photos kept with the right job, not lost in someone's camera roll.

  • Take photos on site from the phone
  • Thumbnails on the job, tap to open full size
  • Photos stay with the job record

Staff permissions

Role-scoped access so each person sees what they need.

  • Admins control who sees what
  • Keep rates and quotes to authorised roles
  • Crew see their own jobs and paperwork

Guided quotes

Step through each quote the same way every time.

  • A consistent step-by-step quoting process
  • Less chance of leaving something off
  • Quotes restricted to authorised roles

Team chat

One shared conversation for crew-wide updates and questions.

  • Crew-wide updates in one place
  • Fewer group texts and phone calls

Timesheets & leave

Crew log hours and request leave from their phone.

  • Hours entered on the phone
  • Leave requests in the app
  • Admins review in one place

Prestarts & SWMS

Prestart checks and SWMS on the phone, not in the glovebox.

  • Prestart checks done in the app
  • SWMS kept with the work
  • Records in one place

Fleet & servicing

Keep track of machines and vehicles and when they're due for a service.

  • List of plant and vehicles
  • Servicing records and due dates

Reimbursements

Staff submit out-of-pocket costs for approval.

  • Submit a claim from the phone
  • Admins review and approve
