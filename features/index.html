Mission · Features

Everything the crew needs, on the phone.

Every feature is included in the one plan.

Records export as CSV files.

Weekly planner on desktop

Set up the crew's whole week from the office computer, with crew down the side and days across the top.

From the office computer, the weekly planner shows the crew down the side and the days of the week across the top. You assign jobs, people and machines to each day. Changes show up on everyone's phone.

Jobs & run sheets

Plan the work and put the day's run sheet on every phone.

Each job keeps the details, address and scope in one place. The day's run sheet is on every phone, in order, so the crew can see the jobs they are on and where they are going. The Today screen can also show fleet and SWMS reminders.

Job photos

Site photos kept with the right job, not lost in someone's camera roll.

Take photos on site from the phone and they stay with that job, rather than in someone's camera roll. Thumbnails show on the job record, and you can tap one to open it full size.

Staff permissions

Role-scoped access so each person sees what they need.

An admin invites each person as an operator or a supervisor. Operators see their own jobs and paperwork. Rates and quotes stay with authorised roles, and admins control who sees what.

Guided quotes

Step through each quote the same way every time, and review the price before accepting.

Quotes follow the same three steps each time: the job details, the machine and any labour, then a review of the price before you accept. Accepting turns the quote into an unscheduled job. Only authorised roles can see or prepare quotes.

Team chat

One shared conversation for crew-wide updates and questions.

The whole crew shares one conversation for updates and questions. Messages sit in that team chat, instead of being spread across group texts and calls.

Timesheets & leave

Crew log hours and request leave from their phone.

From the phone, crew can clock on or add hours by hand for the week. Leave requests cover annual, sick or other leave, and show whether they are pending or decided. Admins review hours and leave in the same app.

Prestarts & SWMS

Prestart checks and SWMS on the phone, not in the glovebox.

Prestart checks are done in the app against a machine, with pass, fail or not applicable on each item. A fail on a marked item means do not operate. SWMS documents can be read and signed on the phone, and the records stay in one place.

Fleet, servicing & compliance

Track plant and vehicles, servicing, and when rego, COI, WorkCover and insurance are due.

Each machine or vehicle is listed with its registration and, where it needs one, its Queensland certificate of inspection (COI) expiry. You can also record when it was last serviced and when the next service is due, by date or by hours or kilometres. WorkCover and insurance policy renewal dates are kept for admins; staff do not see those policies.

Reimbursements

Staff submit out-of-pocket costs for approval.

Staff can submit a claim from the phone for an out-of-pocket cost, attach a receipt, and see its status. Admins review claims, and can approve, decline or mark them paid.
