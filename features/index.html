Pick one trade or several when you set up your company, and add or remove trades later. Each trade comes with its own job types, job titles and quote pack. Earthworks quotes are priced by machine. The other trades are measured, such as area, linear metres, per hole or per job, and start with default rates you replace with your own.

From the office computer, the weekly planner shows the crew down the side and the days of the week across the top. You assign jobs, people and machines to each day. Changes show up on everyone's phone.

Each job keeps the details, address and scope in one place. The day's run sheet is on every phone, in order, so the crew can see the jobs they are on and where they are going. The Today screen can also show fleet and SWMS reminders.

Take photos on site from the phone and they stay with that job, rather than in someone's camera roll. Thumbnails show on the job record, and you can tap one to open it full size. Uploads show their progress, and if the signal stalls, only the photos that did not save are offered to send by email.

An admin invites each person as a labourer, operator, supervisor, manager or admin, and can give each job title its own permission level. Crew see their own jobs and paperwork. Rates and quotes stay with supervisors, managers and admins, and the crew's job details leave out prices.

Quotes follow the same steps each time: the job details, what the work needs, then a review of the price before you accept. Earthworks steps through the machine and any labour, and the other trades step through their measured items. Accepting turns the quote into a job. Only authorised roles can see or prepare quotes.

The whole crew shares one conversation for updates and questions. Messages sit in that team chat, instead of being spread across group texts and calls.

From the phone, crew can clock on or add hours by hand for the week. Leave requests cover annual, sick or other leave, and show whether they are pending or decided. Admins review hours and leave in the same app.

Prestart checks are done in the app against a machine, with pass, fail or not applicable on each item. A fail on a marked item means do not operate. SWMS documents can be read and signed on the phone, and the records stay in one place.

Each machine or vehicle is listed with its registration and, where it needs one, its Queensland certificate of inspection (COI) expiry. You can also record when it was last serviced and when the next service is due, by date or by hours or kilometres. WorkCover and insurance policy renewal dates are kept for admins; staff do not see those policies.

Staff can submit a claim from the phone for an out-of-pocket cost, attach a receipt, and see its status. Admins review claims, and can approve, decline or mark them paid.

New in CallUp

CallUp is GPS-enabled. While a crew member is clocked on and has CallUp open, their phone shares its GPS position about once a minute, or sooner if they move more than 50 metres. Managers and admins see a live crew map on the home screen with each clocked-on person's name, when their phone last updated, and today's job. Only the latest position is kept, with no trail or route, and clocking off removes it. One location is also saved on the timesheet at Clock on, Clock off, Break start and Break end. Crew can say no to location in their browser and still clock on. A phone browser stops sharing when the phone is locked or CallUp isn't on screen. Supervisors and crew don't see the map.

Quotes open on your first trade and you can switch to another. Earthworks steps through the machine, any labour and extras. Measured trades price by quantity, for example square metres for tiling, linear metres for fencing, or metres sawn and holes cored for concrete cutting. The rates screen shows a section for each trade you picked, and a rate you save replaces that pack's default.

Tick each category the job needs and it becomes a section with its own measurements or machines. The quote shows a subtotal per section and one ex-GST, GST and inc-GST total. A Quote accepted button sits at the top of every quote, and accepting can make one job or one job per section. Each changed save keeps a numbered revision you can compare before you accept.

The office can attach PDFs, images, Word and Excel files, and CAD drawings to a job, up to 25 MB each. Each document is either Office only or Crew can see. Crew assigned to the job open only the files marked Crew can see, so quotes and invoices can stay with the office. A document added with no signal uploads once the phone is back in range.

Instead of every job starting at 7:00am, CallUp spaces the day's jobs from your company's start time using an estimate for each job: the quote's hours, then the usual clocked time for that job type, then a default table you can edit. A short job can fill an earlier gap. The finish time defaults to 5:00pm, and a job that runs past it stays on the board with a flag. A start time someone set by hand is left alone.

Supervisors and above get a week view built from job bookings and approved leave, showing who is free, who is booked and who is on approved leave, so you can see where there is room before you book more work.

Crew step through Stop and think, Look for hazards, Assess the risk, Control the hazards, and Proceed safely when they start a job. A short note is required and a photo is optional. Take 5s are on for every company until an admin switches them off.

Managers and admins see a ranked table on the Staff tab, built from hours worked, jobs completed, prestart and Take 5 completion, incidents and sick days. You set how much each one counts, and the score runs from 0 to 100 within the list. Crew and supervisors do not see it.

Each person can turn on notifications on their phone. A new team chat message notifies the rest of the company, and at 6am Brisbane time each person is sent the jobs they are on that day.

The sign-in screen has a Forgot password link that emails a reset link. A company admin can also email a reset link to another login in their own company.
